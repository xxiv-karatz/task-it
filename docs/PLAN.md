# Task-IT Plan

Task-IT will begin as a web-only service marketplace for the South African market, with Pan-African expansion considered after the web business has validated demand.

## MVP Features

### Customer-Facing

- Customer account creation and sign-in
- Browse and search service categories
- View provider profiles, service details, availability, verification status, and reviews
- Request or book a service time
- Secure in-app payment with escrow for supported bookings
- In-app messaging with providers
- Booking status and history
- Leave reviews and ratings after completed services

### Provider-Facing

- Provider account creation and onboarding
- Provider profile with service categories, coverage area, pricing, availability, and portfolio details
- Identity and profile verification workflow
- Manage booking requests, schedules, and service status
- In-app messaging with customers
- Payment and earnings history for completed bookings
- Receive customer reviews and ratings

### Platform/Admin

- Manage customers, providers, categories, and service listings
- Review and manage provider verification
- Monitor bookings, payments, escrow states, refunds, and disputes
- Moderate profiles, messages, reviews, and reported content
- Basic marketplace reporting and operational dashboard

## Rollout Phases

### Phase 1: Single-City Lifestyle MVP

Launch a web app in one South African city with lifestyle and personal services. Validate customer demand, provider onboarding, booking completion, payments, and repeat usage.

### Phase 2: Maintenance and Expansion

Add maintenance and technical services with provider subscriptions and customer-provider pricing. Expand to more cities while remaining web-only.

### Phase 3: Native Apps and Pan-African Expansion

Build native iOS and Android apps and expand across African markets once web traction and the operating model justify the investment.

## Open Decisions

- Final frontend, backend, database, and hosting stack
- Payment provider and escrow implementation
- Provider identity and service verification process
- Initial launch city and category prioritization
- Subscription pricing and marketplace policies for Model B
